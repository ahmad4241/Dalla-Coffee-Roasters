Our story

We roast on Tuesdays.

Dalla started with a small drum roaster and one idea: the coffee you drink at home should be as fresh as the coffee we pour at the bar. We named it after the pot every Iraqi home keeps for guests.

Why the dallah

In Iraq, coffee is how you say welcome. The dallah comes out for guests, for weddings, for the long evenings when nobody wants to leave. We wanted a name that carries that.

What we do is specialty coffee: beans we buy by origin, roast in small batches and taste every week. Old welcome, new way of brewing.

From bean to cup

  1. 1
    RoastEvery Tuesday, in small batches, so nothing sits on a shelf for weeks.
  2. 2
    RestBeans rest for five days before they're sold. Espresso tastes best once the gas settles.
  3. 3
    Grind and pourGround to order, for your machine, your dallah or your V60. At the bar: 18 grams in, 36 out, about 27 seconds.

Questions we hear a lot

How fresh is the coffee?

We roast every Tuesday and sell within three weeks of roasting. The roast date is printed on every bag.

Can you grind it for me?

Yes, for free. Pick espresso, Turkish or Arabic, V60 and filter, or French press when you order. Whole beans stay fresh longest.

Where do you deliver?

Same-day delivery in Karbala for orders before 2 pm, and 2 to 4 days to Baghdad, Najaf, Basra and the rest of Iraq.

How does the Roast Club work?

Choose how often you want coffee. We send fresh bags from that week's roast. Skip, pause or cancel any time from your account.

Do you supply cafés and offices?

Yes. We roast for cafés, restaurants and offices, and we can help set up your espresso machine. Send us a message with Wholesale as the topic.
